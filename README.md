# Aerospace Quantitative Analysis
 
## Project Overview

Why is 45° considered the most important launch angle in projectile motion?

While working on my Rocket Trajectory Simulator project, I became curious about how launch angle affects the distance a rocket can travel. I wanted to understand why some launch angles produce much longer ranges than others, even when the launch speed remains exactly the same.

This small quantitative analysis uses mathematics and Excel to explore the relationship between launch angle and projectile range.

---

## Research Question

How does launch angle influence the horizontal range of a projectile when launch velocity remains constant?
